# Tidy Downloads

The files in this folder are [Tidy Downloads](https://github.com/Vertex-Mods/Zen-Tidy-Downloads)
by Bxthesda and Zylaah, included in Zia with their permission. They belong
to their authors and are not covered by Zia's MIT licence.

Zia loads these scripts itself, in order, only with "Rename finished
downloads with AI" (`zia.features.tidy-downloads`) on in Zia's settings
(`src/js/28l-tidy-downloads.js`); its only change to them is the same check
at the top of `tidy-downloads.uc.js`. Their styles are in
`src/css/26-tidy-downloads.css`.
