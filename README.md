# Zia

**Zen, made calm.**

A [Sine](https://github.com/CosmoCreeper/Sine) mod that reshapes Zen Browser from the frame in.

[Install](#install) · [Highlights](#highlights) · [Settings](#settings) · [Good to know](#good-to-know) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)

</div>

![Zia](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/z1n-k/zia/readme-images/hero.webp)

## Install

1. Install [Sine](https://github.com/CosmoCreeper/Sine).
2. In Zen, open **Settings → Sine Mods**, and in Sine's settings turn on **installing JS from unofficial sources**.
3. Paste `z1n-k/zia` into the box under the marketplace, then restart Zen.

Use Zen's **Sidebar and Top Toolbar** or **Only Sidebar** layout. If Zia doesn't load, open `about:support` and click **Clear startup cache**.

> [!TIP]
> Space and folder icons carry much of the look. [Watch how to set them up](https://vimeo.com/1228144298).

<details>
<summary>Colour and toolbar</summary>

- The colour follows the page as you scroll, and each site's is remembered.
- Prefer another colour for a site? Right-click the toolbar, choose **Toolbar Color for This Site…** and click any part of the page.
- Back, forward and reload are animated.
- Right-click an extension's button to give it one of Zia's icons or your own SVG.

### A quieter address bar

Short rows, one size of text, nothing extra. It sits at the top or the bottom, centred if you like.

<!-- IMAGE address-bar.webp (1600×900): the pop-up open, typing an address -->

<details>
<summary>Address bar details</summary>

- The address reads as `domain / title`.
- As you type an address, the site's icon replaces the magnifying glass.
- Open tabs are marked **Switch to tab**.
- A paperclip copies the page's link.

</details>

### Frosted where it floats

The compact sidebar, hover cards and address pop-up are frosted, the page blurred behind them. The sidebar can let your desktop show through, too.

### Split view, by dragging

Drag a tab over the page and drop cards rise either side. Each site gets its own toolbar.

![Dragging a tab into a split](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/z1n-k/zia/readme-images/split-drag.png)

### Music, in its colours

A player card with the artwork and a soft glow in its colours, and sound bars on playing tabs.

![The music player card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/z1n-k/zia/readme-images/music-card.png)

### Picture-in-picture, out of the way

Just the video until you hover. Throw it at a screen edge, or right-click it, to tuck it away. Tucked, it can peek out in full as you point at it, and a playing video can pop out as you leave its tab.

![Picture-in-picture with its controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/z1n-k/zia/readme-images/pip-controls.webp)

### Four videos, one tab

Right-click a video and choose **Add to Multiview**. Up to four videos or live streams share one tab.

![Multiview with four videos](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/z1n-k/zia/readme-images/multiview-wall.webp)

### And more

- **Bookmarks and History** as a second sidebar beside the page.
- **The Library** drawn the way Zia draws the sidebar.
- **PDFs** in a cleaner viewer.
- **Smart folders:** a model on your machine names new folders and picks their icons.
- **Download names:** finished downloads renamed by AI, with an undo.

<details>
<summary>Smart folders and download names</summary>

**Smart folders.** Set `browser.ml.enable` to `true` in `about:config`, restart Zen, then turn on **Smart folder names**. The first use downloads a model of about 25 MB. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Download names.** Turn on **Rename downloads with AI**, restart Zen, then choose a service and add its key, or use Ollama on your own machine. The service is told the page's address, and for a picture gets a small copy of it. Downloads from private windows are never sent.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Zen settings Zia changes</summary>

Mostly as defaults, so a value you set yourself in `about:config` is kept. The exceptions are noted.

- `zen.widget.mac.mono-window-controls` → off, for macOS's own window buttons.
- `zen.urlbar.replace-newtab` → off, unless **Real new tabs** is off.
- `zen.splitView.enable-tab-drop` → off, unless **Split drop cards** is off.
- `browser.urlbar.trimHttps` → on, unless **Full address** is on.
- `browser.urlbar.untrimOnUserInteraction` → off at every start, and its `.featureGate` off, so the address isn't expanded as you click it.
- `browser.urlbar.suggest.calculator` and `browser.urlbar.unitConversion.enabled` → on.
- `media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.improved-video-controls.enabled` → on.
- `media.videocontrols.picture-in-picture.enable-when-switching-tabs.enabled` follows **Auto picture-in-picture**.
- `zen.theme.acrylic-elements` → off, once, if you hadn't set it.

</details>

## Good to know

- Zia is in beta. It's tested on macOS, Windows and Linux, and looks its best in dark mode.
- **Collapsed Sidebar** isn't supported yet.
- Other mods may conflict. If something looks off, turn them off and add them back one at a time, or [open an issue](https://github.com/z1n-k/zia/issues).

<details>
<summary>Animations not moving?</summary>

Zia follows your system's reduced motion setting. To let Zen animate anyway, add a **Number** setting named `ui.prefersReducedMotion` in `about:config`, set it to `0` and restart Zen.

</details>

<details>
<summary>For developers</summary>

`zia.uc.js` and `chrome.css` are built from the parts in `src/js` and `src/css`, one file per feature. Edit the parts, then run `scripts/build.sh`. `scripts/build.sh --check` runs on every push.

</details>
